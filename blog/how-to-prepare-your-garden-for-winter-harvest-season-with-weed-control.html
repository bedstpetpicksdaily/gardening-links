Frequently Asked Questions

When should I start preparing my garden for winter harvest?

Begin preparation 4-6 weeks before your region's first frost date. This allows time for weed control applications to work effectively and soil amendments to integrate before planting winter crops.

What weed control products from Elixir Garden Supplies work best for winter gardens?

Elixir offers both pre-emergent herbicides for prevention and post-emergent solutions for existing weeds. Choose based on your current weed situation and winter crop requirements. Their staff can recommend specific products for your needs.

Can I use Elixir Garden Supplies fertilizers immediately before planting?

It's best to apply fertilizers 2-3 weeks before planting to allow nutrients to distribute throughout the soil. This timing ensures your winter crops receive balanced nutrition without nutrient burn from concentrated applications.

Are Elixir Garden Supplies weed control products safe for organic winter vegetables?

Elixir offers both conventional and organic-compliant options. Check product labels to confirm certification status if growing for organic standards. Their customer service team can guide you to appropriate solutions.

How often should I apply weed control during winter growing season?

Frequency depends on weed pressure and product type. Pre-emergent applications typically last 8-12 weeks, while post-emergent treatments may need reapplication every 3-4 weeks for persistent weeds. Follow Elixir's product guidelines for best results.
