Frequently Asked Questions

What height privacy screen is best for fall entertaining?

For optimal privacy without creating a closed-in feeling, privacy screens between 5-6 feet tall work well. This height blocks sightlines from neighboring properties while maintaining open sight lines within your entertaining space and allowing autumn breezes to circulate freely.

Can Kozyard hardtop gazebos withstand fall and winter weather?

Yes, Kozyard hardtop gazebos are engineered with durable materials designed to withstand rain, wind, and temperature changes throughout fall and winter. Their solid construction provides year-round protection, making them excellent long-term investments for year-round entertaining.

How should I arrange privacy screens with outdoor furniture?

Position privacy screens around your patio's perimeter to create natural boundaries. Arrange outdoor furniture in conversational groupings within your screened area. Use planters strategically to enhance privacy and add visual interest while maintaining balance and flow throughout your space.

What maintenance do Kozyard outdoor products require in fall?

Regular cleaning to remove fallen leaves and debris, checking hardware for tightness, and applying protective sealants annually helps extend the life of Kozyard products. Most pieces are low-maintenance, requiring only basic seasonal care.
