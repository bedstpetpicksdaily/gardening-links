Elixir Garden Supplies Organic Weed Control

Elixir Garden Supplies offers a range of organic weed control products formulated with acetic acid and plant-based ingredients. Their organic herbicides are OMRI-certified and safe for use around edible crops, making them ideal for gardeners approaching harvest. Application is straightforward: spray directly on weeds on a dry, sunny day. Results typically appear within 24–48 hours, with complete die-back in 5–7 days. At approximately £8–12 per litre, Elixir's products are mid-priced in the organic category. The main advantage is their suitability for organic certification and food safety compliance. However, they require multiple applications on perennial weeds and work best on young, tender weeds rather than established root systems.

Roundup ProPlus (Chemical Alternative)

Roundup ProPlus is a glyphosate-based systemic herbicide that kills weeds from root to leaf. It's the industry standard for quick, comprehensive weed elimination. A single application typically shows visible results within 3–7 days, with complete death of perennial weeds in 10–14 days. Roundup ProPlus costs £5–8 per litre, making it the most affordable option tested. Its systemic action means it tackles deep root systems effectively, and one bottle treats a significantly larger area than organic alternatives. The critical drawback: glyphosate cannot be used near edible crops within 14 days of harvest in most regions, and residue concerns make it unsuitable for organic growers or those prioritizing food safety during peak harvest season.

Neudorff Organic Weed Control

Neudorff specializes in natural garden care, offering products based on fatty acids and clove oil extract. Their organic herbicide is fast-acting by organic standards, with visible wilting in 24 hours and complete die-back in 7–10 days. Neudorff products cost £10–14 per litre, positioning them at the premium end of the organic market. They're particularly effective on annual weeds and young perennials, and their natural formulation is safe for immediate harvest use. However, like other organic herbicides, they require repeat applications on established perennial weeds and are less cost-effective than chemical alternatives for large-scale weed problems.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use chemical weed killers just before harvest?

Most chemical herbicides, including Roundup, require a 14–21 day interval between application and harvest of edible crops. Check your product label and local regulations. Organic herbicides like Elixir's have no harvest interval, making them safer for last-minute weed management.

Why do organic herbicides need multiple applications?

Organic herbicides work by burning down foliage and stems. They don't translocate through root systems like glyphosate does. Perennial weeds can regrow from intact roots, requiring repeat applications—typically 2–3 times per season for heavy infestations.

Is Elixir Garden Supplies cheaper than Roundup and Neudorff?

Elixir (£8–12/L) falls between Roundup ProPlus (£5–8/L, cheapest) and Neudorff (£10–14/L, most expensive). However, Roundup covers more area per application, so cost per square metre favours Roundup—but only if harvest-timing restrictions don't apply to your situation.

Which works best on perennial weeds like docks and thistles?

Roundup ProPlus's systemic action kills perennial roots in a single application. Organic herbicides (Elixir and Neudorff) require 2–4 applications spread across weeks. For established perennials, chemical herbicides are more efficient—but time applications well before harvest.

Are organic herbicides truly safe for harvest-season use?

Yes. Elixir's OMRI-certified products break down quickly in soil and leave no synthetic residue. They're safe to use the day before harvest. Always verify product certification and follow label directions for your specific crops.
