Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between bypass and anvil pruning shears?

Bypass shears use two curved blades that pass each other like scissors, making clean cuts ideal for healthy branches. Anvil shears have one sharp blade that cuts against a flat surface, better suited for dead wood. Bypass shears are generally preferred for live plants.

How often should I sharpen my pruning tool blades?

Sharp blades should be maintained regularly—typically monthly during heavy use seasons. Dull blades crush plant tissue instead of cutting cleanly, increasing disease risk. Many quality pruning tools from Garden Guru Tools feature easily replaceable blades for convenience.

What size pruning tool do I need for thick branches?

Hand pruners typically handle branches up to 1/2 inch diameter. Loppers manage 1/2 to 1.5 inches, while pruning saws are necessary for anything larger. Choosing appropriately-sized tools prevents strain and ensures clean cuts.

Are ergonomic handles really worth the extra cost?

Yes. Ergonomic handles significantly reduce hand fatigue and repetitive stress injuries during extended use. For fall cleanup sessions lasting hours, the comfort investment pays dividends in reduced pain and better cutting technique.

Complete sets from Garden Guru Tools offer better value if you need multiple tool types. However, purchasing individual quality tools allows you to customize your collection based on specific needs and budget constraints.
