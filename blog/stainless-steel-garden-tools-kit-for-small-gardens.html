    Are stainless steel garden tools worth the extra cost?

    Yes, stainless steel tools are worth the investment because they resist rust and corrosion, lasting 2-3 times longer than carbon steel alternatives. They require minimal maintenance and won't stain your hands or plants, making them ideal for frequent gardeners.

    What tools should be included in a small garden kit?

    A complete small garden kit should include a hand trowel, cultivator, pruning shears, weeder, and garden fork—these five tools handle 95% of small-space gardening tasks. Some kits also add a dibber and transplanter for seed starting and potting work.

    Stainless steel requires minimal care—simply rinse tools after use and dry them to prevent water spots, then store in a dry location. Occasionally wipe blades with a cloth and apply light oil to moving joints to keep them functioning smoothly.
