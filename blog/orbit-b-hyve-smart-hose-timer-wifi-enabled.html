Frequently Asked Questions

Does the B-hyve really skip watering on rainy days, or is that just marketing?

It's legitimately functional—the timer pulls local weather data and actually prevents watering when rain is forecast or has recently occurred. This means fewer soggy plants and genuine water savings. However, it relies on internet connectivity, so if your WiFi drops, it reverts to your programmed schedule without weather awareness. Not foolproof, but noticeably smarter than timers without this feature.

What happens to the timer if my WiFi goes out?

It doesn't become a brick. The B-hyve falls back to whatever schedule you programmed into it locally—essentially functioning like a dumb timer until connectivity returns. You just lose remote control and weather features during the outage. This is actually a strength compared to some smart devices that require constant WiFi, but it's worth testing your backyard signal strength before buying if WiFi reliability is spotty at your home.

Is the app worth using, or does it feel like bloated software?

The app is genuinely usable—cleaner than most smart home competitors. You can set up custom schedules, adjust run times from your phone, and view watering history without navigating ten menus. September's unpredictable weather means you'll actually use the app to skip waterings or extend them based on temperature, not just in theory. That said, if your phone dies or you rarely check notifications, a physical timer knob might suit you better.
