Frequently Asked Questions

What pruning tools do I need for fall gardening?

For fall, you'll need bypass pruners for live stems, anvil pruners for deadwood, a pruning saw for thick branches, and hedge shears for shrubs. Garden Guru Tools offers all these options in one trusted brand.

How often should I sharpen my pruning tools?

Sharpen your pruning tools every 2-4 weeks during active gardening season, or whenever you notice the blade tearing instead of slicing cleanly. Garden Guru Tools provides sharpening guides and replacement blades for convenience.

Are Garden Guru Tools worth the investment?

Yes. Quality pruning tools from Garden Guru Tools last 10+ years with proper care, making them more economical than replacing cheap tools annually. The precision cuts also prevent plant diseases.

What's the difference between bypass and anvil pruners?

Bypass pruners have two curved blades that pass each other like scissors—ideal for live, green stems. Anvil pruners have one blade that cuts against a flat surface and are better for dead wood. Garden Guru offers both types.
