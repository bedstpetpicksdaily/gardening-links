Frequently Asked Questions

How does this hold up to freezing temperatures and winter?

The resin wicker doesn't crack in cold weather based on my testing, but I'd recommend draining any moisture pooling on the lid before a hard freeze. The structural integrity held through temperature swings in late September when nights dropped into the 40s. Harsh winters with salt spray or sustained ice might be tougher—if you're in a coastal area, verify drainage holes are clear.

Will this last more than two seasons?

Probably yes for normal use, possibly questionable if it's exposed to constant direct sun and temperature extremes year-round. The material isn't as robust as solid plastic storage bins, but the resin weave resists UV degradation better than cheaper alternatives. I'd expect 3-4 reliable seasons with minimal fading.

Can you store heavy items like bags of soil or gravel?

Not ideally. The 50-pound weight capacity means a single 50-pound bag of garden soil maxes it out, and stacking weight inside puts stress on the hinges. This box excels with lighter, organized items: tools, gloves, seeds, lightweight hoses. If you're storing bulk materials, a wooden chest or metal cabinet is better.
