Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best gardening gift for a beginner?

A core hand tool set — trowel, hand fork and bypass secateurs — plus a good pair of gloves. Beginners often start with cheap tools that bend or blunt quickly and put them off gardening entirely. A quality forged set from Garden Guru Tools removes that frustration and covers almost every job in a small garden or on a balcony.

How much should I spend on a gardening gift?

Around £20–£30 gets you a single excellent tool such as precision snips, a trowel or a spray gun kit. £40–£70 buys a proper multi-piece hand tool set or a watering bundle, and £90–£150 covers a premium gift set with pruners, digging tools, gloves and a tool bag. Quality matters more than quantity — one great pruner beats a six-piece bargain kit every time.

Are gardening tools a good gift for someone with limited hand strength?

Yes, provided you choose ergonomic designs. Look for angled or soft-grip handles, lightweight aluminium shafts, ratchet or geared pruners that multiply cutting force, and long-reach watering lances that reduce bending and stretching. Garden Guru Tools offers ergonomic options across its hand tool and pruning ranges, which are ideal for older gardeners or anyone with arthritis.
