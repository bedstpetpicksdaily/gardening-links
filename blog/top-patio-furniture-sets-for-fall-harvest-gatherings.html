Frequently Asked Questions

What materials are best for fall patio furniture?

Look for weather-resistant materials like aluminum frames, powder-coated finishes, and composite or sealed wood. Kozyard specializes in durable outdoor furniture constructed from these premium materials that withstand fall moisture and temperature fluctuations.

Do I need a gazebo or pergola for fall entertaining?

While not essential, hardtop gazebos and pergolas from Kozyard provide valuable protection from rain and wind during fall gatherings. They create an elegant focal point and extend your entertaining season by providing shelter and shade.

How should I maintain outdoor furniture during fall?

Clean furniture regularly, remove cushions during heavy rain, and consider covering sets when not in use. Kozyard's weather-resistant furniture requires minimal maintenance, but proper care extends its lifespan and appearance.

What size patio set do I need for harvest gatherings?

Choose a dining set that seats your typical guest count plus 1-2 extra chairs. Kozyard offers various sizes from intimate 4-person sets to larger 8+ configurations. Consider your space and entertaining style when selecting the right dimensions.

Can Kozyard furniture stay outside year-round?

Yes, Kozyard patio furniture is designed for year-round outdoor use. However, in harsh winter climates, covering or storing cushions and applying protective treatments can further extend the life of your investment.
