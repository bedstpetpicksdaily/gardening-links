Frequently Asked Questions

How does the SILENO life handle wet grass and rain?

It handles light moisture fine, but heavy rain is problematic. I tested it after a moderate overnight rain and it mowed successfully but sluggishly. The manual recommends avoiding saturated grass (which makes sense—any mower struggles then). The app will trigger rain sensors that pause operation during heavy downpours, which is sensible. Plan mowing schedules for drier periods.

Is 750sqm coverage realistic or marketing exaggeration?

It's honest, but with caveats. The 750sqm rating assumes regular mowing (every 2-3 days) on relatively flat terrain. My 750sqm lawn with moderate slope got complete coverage on that schedule. Skip a week and it struggles—grass grows too tall between sessions, and the mower can't mulch effectively. Think of the square meter rating as ideal conditions, not worst-case capacity.

What's the real maintenance commitment required?

More than you'd hope. Blade maintenance every 2-3 months (roughly €15-20 per set), boundary wire inspection after storms, and occasional debris removal from the charging dock. The app doesn't eliminate all responsibility—you're trading active mowing for passive monitoring. Budget 30 minutes monthly for basic upkeep.

Can it handle slopes and uneven ground like mine?

Up to about 20-25 degree slopes, yes. Beyond that, wheel slip becomes an issue. I tested it on my 15-degree slope and it performed admirably. Test first on your specific terrain if slope is a concern—some lawn irregularities (hidden rocks, deep ruts) can confuse the navigation sensors.

Is the WiFi app control actually useful or just convenient?

Genuinely useful. Real-world example: I was away during a heat wave and the app alerted me that the mower had stopped working (boundary wire damaged). I could troubleshoot remotely and fix it before returning home. The scheduling intelligence also learns your lawn's growth patterns and adjusts frequency—that's worth the connected feature alone.
