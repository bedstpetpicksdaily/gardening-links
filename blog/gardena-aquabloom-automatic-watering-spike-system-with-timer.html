Frequently Asked Questions

How often do I actually need to refill the water globe?

In my experience during late summer heat (80-90°F), I refilled every 2-3 days for potted plants. In cooler September weather, it stretched to 4 days. The globe holds roughly 800ml depending on the model, so capacity is honest but not generous. If you're away for a week, you'll need either multiple spikes per plant or a supplementary water source like a soaker hose on a separate timer.

Will this work in my raised garden beds or only containers?

It works in both, but it shines in containers and dedicated planting areas. In my raised beds, I use it selectively—one spike for heat-stressed plants like basil or eggplant, one for peppers. For broad-based watering across the entire bed, drip irrigation is more efficient. The spike system is better for targeted, plant-specific watering.

Is the timer accurate, and can I really trust it in rain?

The timer is reliable and mechanical—no batteries to die mid-summer. During three rainy days in late September, I manually stopped watering and the system didn't malfunction when I restarted it. That said, it's not a smart timer that pauses during rain. You'll need to manually override it in wet periods, which means you can't truly set it and forget it during unpredictable fall weather.
